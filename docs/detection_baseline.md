## Headline (test, measured)

Read these numbers together with the three validity caveats:

1. **Incomplete GT.** SUN 2D labels were not made for COCO. A correct detection of an unlabelled object (a book, a cup, a laptop) counts as a false positive, so precision and AP are **lower bounds**, especially for small desk objects.
2. **Class mismatch.** The SUN-to-COCO mapping is not exact. SUN `table` includes coffee and side tables, and SUN `sofa_chair` (armchair) is mapped to COCO `couch`, although YOLO mostly calls armchairs `chair` (see Class confusion).
3. **Domain shift.** The model is COCO-pretrained (mostly internet photos) and is **not fine-tuned**. SUN images are indoor shots from 4 depth sensors with different image quality.

## Head ablation: one2one (NMS-free) vs one2many + NMS

The same weights and test images. Each head's operating threshold was chosen on its own val run.

| Head | mAP | AP50 | AR@100 | conf_op (val) | P @ op | R @ op | F1 @ op |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| one2one (default) | 0.228 | 0.322 | 0.615 | 0.2411 | 0.544 | 0.485 | 0.513 |
| one2many + NMS (IoU 0.7) | 0.226 | 0.320 | 0.562 | 0.2338 | 0.510 | 0.520 | 0.515 |

mAP differs by 0.002 and F1 at the operating point by 0.003, which is within what one would expect from noise on this data. The NMS head trades precision for recall at its own threshold. **one2one stays the default:** equal accuracy, higher AR@100, and no NMS step to port to ONNX in Phase 06.

## Latency (CPU, measured)

Power: plugged in = True, Windows **Balanced** plan; the machine was also under memory pressure (~0.8 GB free RAM). **Decode** (JPEG read) is reported separately and is not part of detector latency. ## Gallery review (spec 02.2 AC5)

Overlays: `benchmarks/detection/gallery/` (green = prediction at conf_operational, orange = SUN GT of mapped classes). Patterns seen:

- Dense rows of chairs (lecture halls, e.g. `sun_kv2_001208`) are almost entirely missed. The many chairs in these scenes contribute to the 47% of chair GT with no matching detection, and kv2 (which has the most such scenes) has the lowest per-sensor mAP.
- Armchairs are detected as `chair` where SUN says `couch` (`sun_realsense_004894`, `sun_kv2_000326`, `sun_kv1_002649`). The class-confusion table quantifies this.
- Unlabelled-but-correct detections are common: books, papers, a laptop next to a GT `tv` (`sun_kv2_001208`). These are counted as FPs (caveat 1).
- Real errors: a waste bin called `cup` (`sun_realsense_004840`), a teddy bear called `person` (TUM fr3), and large objects cut by the image border missed (`sun_xtion_004165`, GT tv).
- The one2one head occasionally keeps two overlapping boxes of one class (e.g. two `book` boxes in TUM fr1), as noted in spec 02.1.
- On TUM, desk objects are detected consistently (keyboard, mouse, tv, laptop, book, bottle, chair), which is the input Phase 04 tracking needs.
